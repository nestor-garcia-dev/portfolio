# Project write-up template

Use these prompts to write a project README or case study. Replace the prompts with concrete facts before featuring the project. Omit sections that do not apply.

## Overview

State the product name, the problem it solves, its audience, and its current status: in development, pilot, production, or completed personal project. State whether source is public or private.

## Problem and outcome

Describe the previous workflow and what changed. Include only verified outcomes. Give measurement periods and distinguish user-reported figures from measurements collected by the application.

## My role

Explain your contribution, collaborators, and operational responsibilities. If you used AI, describe how you directed and validated the work.

## Product walkthrough

Explain one useful workflow. Add reviewed screenshots with descriptive alt text when available. Use fictional records and identities. A video or live demo is optional.

## Engineering decisions

Explain two or three consequential choices, their tradeoffs, and why they fit this project. Link to deeper architecture documentation rather than repeating it.

## Verification and operations

For public source, document prerequisites, installation, tests, and a runnable example. For private products, describe the verification approach without exposing operational details or making unsupported reliability claims.

## Current status and links

Separate implemented behavior from future plans. Link to source, documentation, examples, or the public case study as appropriate. Do not direct visitors to a customer's production login as a demo.
